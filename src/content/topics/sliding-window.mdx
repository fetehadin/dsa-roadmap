A **train window**: as the train moves, one new tree appears on the right and one old tree disappears on the left. You don't need to look at the whole view again.

### Fixed window (size k)

Compute the first window's sum once. Then for each step: `sum += a[r] − a[r − k]`. That is **O(n)** instead of O(n·k).

### Variable window

Two pointers `l` and `r` mark the window. Move `r` to **grow** it. Whenever the window breaks a rule (a repeated letter, a sum too big), move `l` to **shrink** it until it is valid again. Each index enters once and leaves once, so the total is **O(n)**, even with the inner `while`.

> Built on arrays (Phase 2) and two pointers. Optional.
