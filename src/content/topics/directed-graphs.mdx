On Facebook, friendship goes **both ways**. On Instagram, you can **follow** someone who does not follow you back. The first is an undirected graph; the second is a **directed graph** (digraph). Every edge is an **arrow** from u to v.

### What changes

- **Degree splits in two.** **Out-degree** = arrows leaving a vertex; **in-degree** = arrows arriving.
- **Paths follow the arrows.** 0 → 1 does not let you go from 1 to 0.
- **Storage**: a directed edge u → v is stored **once**:

```cpp
m[u][v] = 1;           // not m[v][u]
adj[u].push_back(v);   // not adj[v]
```

So the matrix is usually **not symmetric**. Its row sum is the out-degree, and its column sum is the in-degree.

### DAGs

A **D**irected **A**cyclic **G**raph has no directed cycles. Course prerequisites, task schedules and build systems are DAGs: you can always find an order that respects every arrow.
