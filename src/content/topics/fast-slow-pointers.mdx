Two runners on a track: the **hare** runs twice as fast as the **tortoise**.

- On a **straight road**, when the hare reaches the finish, the tortoise is exactly **halfway**. That finds the **middle** of a linked list.
- On a **circular track**, the hare eventually **laps** the tortoise and they meet. That detects a **cycle**.
- Give one runner an **n-step head start** at the same speed: when the leader finishes, the other is **n from the end**.

All three need only two pointers: **O(1) extra space**, one pass.
